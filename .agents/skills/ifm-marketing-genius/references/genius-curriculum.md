# IFM marketing genius curriculum

Coach mode. One module per session unless they ask for a map. Teach in
plain English, then drill. Do not lecture all 12 modules.

After each module, offer the next number. Skip ahead if they name a topic.

## How to teach

1. **Law** — the non-negotiable (1–2 sentences)
2. **IFM example** — Vixxo-shaped, not a SaaS metaphor
3. **Common failure** — what amateurs do
4. **Drill** — they rewrite, choose, or kill something
5. **You grade** — score against messaging principles if they answer

If they want to be a genius, **quiz them**. Correct specifically.

## Modules

### 1. Category and shelf
Law: If they think you are software or a CRE generalist, you already lost.
Drill: Write Vixxo's shelf in 20 words. Name the next-best alternative.

### 2. The committee
Law: One message cannot close five vetoes.
Drill: Take any Vixxo homepage sentence and write a facilities version and a
procurement version.

### 3. Buying triggers
Law: Market events, not job titles.
Drill: List 5 accounts (hypothetical banners OK if no CRM) and the trigger
that would make ABM ethical and useful.

### 4. Proof architecture
Law: Claims without mechanisms are ads. Mechanisms without numbers are
hypotheses.
Drill: Kill 3 unscoped claims and replace each with a mechanism + proof gap.

### 5. Demand capture vs demand create
Law: Harvest RFPs, search, and AI answers before you fund thought leadership.
Drill: Sort 6 content ideas into capture vs create. Cut two.

### 6. AEO for FM
Law: AI engines cite extractable, specific, citable pages.
Handoff: `vixxo-profound-aeo` + `ai-seo`.
Drill: Write a 50-word answer-first intro for one real prompt (from Profound
or a guessed high-intent query labeled as guessed).

### 7. ABM physics
Law: Reach the champion *and* the blocker in a trigger window.
Handoff: `ads` ABM/LinkedIn playbooks.
Drill: Outline a 3-touch sequence for facilities + procurement on a cost-out
trigger. No "just checking in."

### 8. Enablement is the product
Law: Marketing wins in rooms it does not attend.
Handoff: `sales-enablement`.
Drill: Write the 5-line internal forward email a facilities champion sends
to procurement.

### 9. Competitive narrative
Law: Contrast operating models, not brand size.
Handoff: `competitors`, `product-marketing`.
Drill: One paragraph vs a CRE giant that does not say cheaper or nicer.

### 10. Offers and CTAs
Law: CTA must match awareness. Demo is late-stage.
Handoff: `offers`, `lead-magnets`.
Drill: Replace "Book a demo" on a category page with a better IFM offer.

### 11. Measurement
Law: MQLs lie; pipeline and RFP inclusion do not.
Handoff: `analytics`, `attribution`.
Drill: Pick 3 leading and 2 lagging metrics for next quarter. Justify kills.

### 12. Integration (genius test)
Law: You can sit the council, still ship something an AE will use Monday.
Handoff: `marketing-council` only if they want debate; then synthesize to
one enablement asset.
Drill: In 15 minutes, produce: one capture page outline, one LinkedIn post
for a facilities director, one procurement objection line. Grade all three.

## Completion

When they finish 1–12, they are not done — they can run Brief weekly and
Critique on real work. Genius in this market is **reps using your assets
and buyers finding you at the moment of pain**.
