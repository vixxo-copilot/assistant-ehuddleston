# IFM channel playbook

Where to spend attention. Default: **capture, enable, then create**.

## Priority order (Vixxo-shaped IFM)

1. **Answer engines + search** — buyers type problems, not brand names.
   Own "national facilities / IFM / multi-site HVAC / restaurant refrigeration"
   style prompts. Route execution through `vixxo-profound-aeo`.
2. **RFP / sales enablement** — language packs, objection docs, one-pagers.
   This is the close path. `sales-enablement`, `competitors`.
3. **LinkedIn ABM** — named accounts in trigger events. Facilities +
   procurement pairs. `ads` (ABM + LinkedIn B2B), `social` only when the post
   is committee-useful.
4. **Association / trade content** — IFMA, NRF, vertical ops. Depth over
   volume. `content-strategy`, `public-relations`.
5. **Events** — booths are optional; meetings with triggered accounts are not.
6. **Email to known accounts** — nurture with operational briefs, not
   newsletters. `emails`. Draft-then-approve if sending.
7. **Paid search** — high-intent category terms; watch CPL vanity. `ads`
   Google Search playbook with IFM quality scoring.
8. **Everything else** — justify or kill. Meta prospecting, TikTok, influencer,
   and "viral creative" are usually the wrong physics.

## Channel physics

| Channel | What it is good at | How it fails in IFM |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AEO / SEO | Mental availability when pain hits | Thin pages that giants out-cite |
| ABM | Account timing | Spray-and-pray job-title lists |
| LinkedIn organic | Champion language | Thought-leadership with no proof |
| Events | Trust, 90-day implementation talks | Swag and brand wallpaper |
| Case studies | Short-list proof | Logos without operational numbers |
| Webinars | Procurement + facilities together | Product tours nobody finishes |
| Cold email | Trigger-based entry | Generic "quick question" SDR sludge |

## Offers that match the motion

Good offers: coverage assessment, RFP checklist, "IFM vs in-house" brief,
90-day transition plan, spend-variance diagnostic.

Weak offers: free tote, ungated 40-page ebook, "book a demo" as the only CTA.

Demo is a **validation-stage** ask. Using it as the only TOFU CTA filters out
people who are still framing the category.

## Budget heuristic (directional, not a finance model)

For a sales-led IFM motion with a finite TAM:

- Enablement + AEO/content (capture): largest share of *marketing labor*
- ABM + high-intent search: largest share of *paid*
- Events: concentrated on trigger accounts, not "presence"
- Brand campaigns: only after proof architecture exists

If paid is scaling while AEs still lack a procurement one-pager, stop paid
until enablement exists.

## Measurement

**Leading (this month):** accounts reached in target list, AE asset usage,
Profound citations/visibility on priority prompts, high-intent demo requests,
RFP language reuse.

**Lagging (this quarter):** sourced and influenced pipeline, short-list rate,
win rate vs named alternative, expansion in existing banners.

Do not optimize IFM paid to MQL volume.
