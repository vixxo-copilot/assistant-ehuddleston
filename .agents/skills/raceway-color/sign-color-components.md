# Sign color components — full checklist

Every illuminated channel-letter sign may need colors across **these
components**. The skill extracts and reports each separately — do not collapse
into one "paint color."

| # | Component | Material | Color source | Site paint? |
|---|-----------|----------|--------------|-------------|
| 1 | **Letter faces** | 3/16" acrylic / Plex | Stock `#` per word/element | No |
| 2 | **Face overlays** | Perforated vinyl, 3M film | Spec on art (e.g. 70/30 black perf) | No |
| 3 | **Returns** | .050 aluminum | Factory pre-finish (usually black) | No |
| 4 | **Trim cap** | Vinyl / alum cap | Factory black (Sally SB30) | No |
| 5 | **Raceway** | 5"×7" extruded alum | SW / PMS / BM — match mount surface | **Yes** |
| 6 | **Fascia / sign band** | EIFS, panel, stucco | SW / PMS / BM on survey or art | **Yes** |
| 7 | **Sign pocket / panel** | Recessed panel | May differ from outer frame | **Yes** |
| 8 | **Wall / building** | Stucco, cement, Dryvit | SW / PMS / BM elevation legend | **Yes** |
| 9 | **Beam / canopy** | Architectural steel, soffit | Often BM or custom SW mix | **Yes** |
| 10 | **Cabinet / pan face** | Aluminum pan, flex face | Paint or vinyl per art | Varies |
| 11 | **Pylon / monument** | Aluminum, ACM | Field paint per elevation | **Yes** |
| 12 | **Border / accent trim** | Alum trim strip | Separate line on art if present | **Yes** |

**Not colors:** LED module part# (PL-FS2-RR1-P), wire, fasteners.

## Output sections (full sign color sheet)

1. Letter faces (by element name)
2. Films & vinyl overlays
3. Factory finishes (returns, trim cap)
4. Field paint (one row per surface: fascia, raceway, wall, pocket, beam, canopy)
5. Part# + client defaults when art is sparse
6. Gaps / visual-read flags
