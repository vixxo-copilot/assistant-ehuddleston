## Purpose, promise, and positioning

**Purpose:** To keep facilities, providers, and local communities running at their best.

**Promise:** Spend less. Stress less. One work order at a time.

**Vision:** Achieve the "Triple Win" — scale, provider volume, and customer FM savings.

**Values (inform tone):**

- Execute with urgency — downtime costs time and money
- Do what's right — no overcharges, no junk fees
- Make your impact BIG — challenge the status quo
- Win Together — collaboration across Vixxo, providers, customers
- Wow the customer — every work ticket is a chance to save time, money, peace of mind

---

## Audience (default for HubSpot content)

- **Primary:** VP+ nationwide retail and multi-site facilities management
- **Secondary:** Facilities directors, procurement, operations leaders evaluating FM partners
- **Not for:** Residential homeowners, DIY audiences, generic "small business" framing

---

## Tone and voice

- Professional, direct, and confident — not casual or hype-driven
- Evidence-backed; cite outcomes and scale where possible
- Urgent when discussing downtime, cost, or risk — aligned with "Execute with urgency"
- Collaborative — "Win Together" — not adversarial toward providers or customers
- Optimistic and clear — match editorial imagery guidelines (bright, straightforward, modern)
- Sentence case for headlines and body (proper nouns excepted)
- Left-aligned prose; avoid all caps for emphasis

## Banned phrases and framing

- Residential HVAC, homeowner, "around the house," DIY
- Unverified superlatives: "best in the world," "#1," "guaranteed" without substantiation
- Fear-mongering without a constructive path forward
- Overcharges, junk fees, hidden fees — as accusations against Vixxo (we stand against these)
- All-caps headlines or body text
- Hype language: "game-changing," "disruptive," "revolutionary" without proof
- Generic stock phrases that could apply to any FM vendor

## CTA patterns

Default CTAs should connect to the brand promise (less spend, less stress, one work order at a time):

- **Learn more** — low commitment, editorial content
- **Download the [guide/playbook/assessment]** — lead-gen content
- **Request a portfolio assessment** — high-intent FM buyers
- **Talk to our team** — nurture / late stage

CTA tone: clear action verb + specific outcome. Avoid vague "Click here."

## Channel notes

### Blog
- Lead with audience pain (downtime, cost, scale complexity)
- Support with portfolio/multi-site proof points
- One primary CTA per post

### Marketing email
- Subject: specific benefit or question; avoid clickbait
- Preheader: extends subject, not duplicate
- One header image (Breeze AI) + one primary CTA button

### Social (LinkedIn primary)
- Hook in first line; professional tone
- Link to gated asset or blog; one Breeze image per post
- Hashtags sparingly; prefer clear copy over tag stuffing
